## Share text

Every identity vendor now has an AI agent story. They aren't telling the same one.

Sit through five briefings and you'll hear five different problems: register the agent, remove its standing privilege, discover and certify it, or get delegation right token by token.

My read: each vendor is approaching agents from the chair it already sits in. Directory, vault, governance, or gateway. That starting point predicts where the product is deep and where it's thin.

So this week I mapped what the major platforms have actually published, plus the standards work quietly happening underneath. Competitors are co-authoring the plumbing, and that should shape how you buy.

Inside the article: a scenario you'll probably recognize, the four starting points, and the one decision to make before any vendor meeting.

Agentic Identity Notes, week 3. If you've already bought something for agent identity, which chair did it come from?
