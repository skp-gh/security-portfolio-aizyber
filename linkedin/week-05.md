"Are we safe from AI agents?" If a director asked you that tomorrow, what number would you put on the screen?

Most security slides answer a different question. They count activity: alerts, patches, tickets. A board can't tell from those whether exposure is going up or down.

So this week I built a scorecard. Ten agent identity KPIs, grouped under four questions a board can remember: do we know what we have, is each agent constrained, can we stop one fast, and can we prove what happened.

The number I'd lead with is time to revoke everywhere. It turns an abstract risk into minutes or hours, and you only get it by running a real drill.

Inside the article: the board-room scenario, the vanity metric trap, and the five KPIs you can measure in your first 30 days.

Agentic Identity Notes, week 5. If you could show your board only one AI agent metric, which would it be?
